Put theneedle down.

Meridian is a belt-driven turntable machined from nine kilograms of aluminium. It plays records, and keeps out of the way while it does.

The cueing lever is oil-damped, so the stylus settles into the lead-in groove instead of dropping onto it.

Most of what you hear from a turntable is the turntable. Meridian is built so the only thing moving is the record.

Every layer is there to keep vibration away from the stylus.

  • Wool felt mat

    3 mm of pressed wool absorbs what the record picks up from the room.

  • Platter

    2.1 kg of turned aluminium, balanced to within a hundredth of a gram so it spins without wobble.

  • Inverted bearing

    A hardened steel shaft turns on a single ceramic ball, running in oil it never needs topped up.

  • Plinth

    Machined from one 9 kg aluminium billet. No joins, no glue, nothing to ring.

  • Isolation feet

    Sorbothane cores tuned below 10 Hz, so footsteps and bass stay out of the groove.

At this distance, a groove is 0.04 mm wide.

The stylus follows it at 1.8 grams of tracking force, for about 480 metres a side.

Change speed with a button, not by moving the belt.

A quartz-locked motor holds both speeds to within 0.1%, and brings the platter up to speed in under two seconds.
