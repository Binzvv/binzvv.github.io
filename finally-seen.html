10  For everyone else

What good allyship looks like.

Listen and learn

Understand how ADHD shows up in women.

  1. Listen and learn. Understand how ADHD shows up in women.
  2. Look beyond appearances. Don't assume someone isn't struggling because they're successful or seem to be coping.
  3. Challenge stereotypes. Speak up when women are labelled difficult, emotional or sensitive.
  4. Respect individual needs. Offer flexibility, clear communication and support without judgement.
  5. Be kind. Sometimes the smallest acts of understanding make the biggest difference.
